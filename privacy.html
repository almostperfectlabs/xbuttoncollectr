XButton Collectr Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026

XButton Collectr ("XButton Collectr," "the app," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use XButton Collectr, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

XButton Collectr is operated by Almost Perfect Labs.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create or use an XButton Collectr account, we may collect information associated with your account, including:

  • Your name or display name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo, if provided by your sign-in provider
  • Account identifier or user ID
  • XButton Collectr friend code
  • Account creation and activity information
  • Profile preferences and visibility settings

If you sign in using a third-party authentication provider such as Google or Apple, that provider may supply XButton Collectr with information necessary to authenticate your account. XButton Collectr does not receive or store your Google or Apple account password.

Collection and App Content

XButton Collectr stores information you choose to enter or import into the app. This may include:

  • Video games in your collection
  • Wishlist items
  • Game condition and completeness information
  • Purchase dates and purchase prices
  • Estimated values
  • Notes and other collection details
  • Badges and profile achievements
  • Featured games
  • Collection statistics
  • Profile and collector preferences

This information is used to provide the app's collection-management, backup, statistics, profile, and social features.

Friends and Social Features

If you use XButton Collectr's social features, we may process information related to:

  • Friend requests
  • Friend relationships
  • User searches
  • Profile information
  • Collection information made visible to friends
  • Featured games and badges
  • Collection and wishlist statistics

The amount of information visible to other users depends on the features of the app and your available privacy or profile settings.

Analytics and App Usage Information

XButton Collectr uses Google Analytics for Firebase to help understand how the app is used and improve its reliability and features.

Depending on device configuration and the Firebase services in use, analytics information may include:

  • App launches and sessions
  • Screen or feature usage
  • App version
  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • General geographic information
  • App-instance and device identifiers
  • Diagnostic and performance-related information
  • Events related to features within XButton Collectr

XButton Collectr may also record analytics events such as account creation, sign-in, game additions or removals, collection-size ranges, backup and restore activity, and use of certain app features.

Analytics information is used to understand app usage, troubleshoot problems, evaluate features, and improve XButton Collectr. We do not use XButton Collectr user information to sell targeted advertising.

Cloud Storage and Backups

XButton Collectr may use Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and related Firebase services, to provide account, cloud backup, synchronization, social, and other app functionality.

Information stored through these services may include account information, collection information, profile settings, badges, friend relationships, wishlist information, and other data needed to provide XButton Collectr features.

Notifications

When notification functionality is available and enabled, XButton Collectr may use Firebase Cloud Messaging or similar services to deliver app notifications.

This may involve processing a device notification token and information necessary to determine which notifications should be delivered to your device. Notification tokens are used to deliver app-related notifications and are not sold to advertisers.

How We Use Information

We use information collected through XButton Collectr to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Store and restore game collections
  • Synchronize information between devices
  • Provide wishlist, badge, profile, and statistics features
  • Enable friend requests and social features
  • Display information that users choose to make visible to friends
  • Provide notifications
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Analyze app usage
  • Improve existing features
  • Develop new features
  • Protect the security and integrity of XButton Collectr

How Information Is Shared

XButton Collectr does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the app, including Google Firebase and related Google services used for authentication, cloud storage, analytics, messaging, and application infrastructure.

Information you intentionally make available through XButton Collectr's social features may also be visible to other XButton Collectr users or friends according to the app's functionality and your profile settings.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, or valid governmental requests, or to protect the rights, security, or integrity of XButton Collectr and its users.

Third-Party Services

XButton Collectr uses or may interact with third-party services that have their own privacy practices. These may include:

  • Google Firebase
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Google Sign-In
  • Sign in with Apple

Your use of those services may also be subject to the privacy policies and terms of the respective providers.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by XButton Collectr.

XButton Collectr uses Firebase and modern encrypted network connections to transmit data between the app and cloud services. Access to cloud-stored user information is restricted using authentication and database security rules intended to prevent unauthorized access.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

XButton Collectr generally retains account and collection information for as long as your account remains active or as necessary to provide the app's services.

If you delete your XButton Collectr account, information associated with the account will be deleted from XButton Collectr's active systems, except where limited retention is reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Anonymous or aggregated analytics information that can no longer reasonably be associated with your account may be retained for statistical and service-improvement purposes.

Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your XButton Collectr account and its associated user data.

Account deletion can be initiated from within XButton Collectr or through our external Account Deletion page:

XButton Collectr Account Deletion

You may also contact us at almostperfectlabs@gmail.com regarding account or privacy questions.

Deleting an XButton Collectr account is intended to permanently remove the account and data associated with that account from XButton Collectr's active systems. Account deletion is not the same as temporarily signing out of the app or uninstalling it.

Certain information may be retained when required for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes.

Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide you with certain rights regarding your personal information, which may include rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain information about the use of your personal information.

You may contact us using the information below to submit a privacy-related request.

We will not discriminate against users for exercising privacy rights provided by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

XButton Collectr is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through XButton Collectr in a manner that requires parental consent or deletion, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

International Users

Information used by XButton Collectr may be processed or stored in countries other than the country in which you live, including through cloud service providers such as Google Firebase.

Privacy and data-protection laws may differ between jurisdictions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when XButton Collectr's features or data practices change or when required to address legal or platform requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will be revised.
