Delete Your XButton Collectr Account

XButton Collectr users may permanently delete their account and associated user data.

Delete Your Account From the App

Open XButton Collectr and navigate to your account or profile settings.

Select Delete Account and follow the confirmation instructions.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Uninstalling XButton Collectr or signing out of the app does not delete your account.

If You Cannot Access the App

If you no longer have access to XButton Collectr, you may request deletion by emailing:

almostperfectlabs@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your XButton Collectr account whenever possible and use XButton Collectr Account Deletion as the subject.

We may need to verify that you are the owner of the account before processing the deletion request.

Data Deleted With Your Account

Account deletion includes data associated with your account, such as:

  • Account and profile information
  • Game collection information
  • Wishlist information
  • Cloud backup information
  • Friend relationships and friend requests
  • Badges and featured profile information
  • User preferences and other account-associated app data

Data That May Be Retained

Information may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or other legitimate legal obligations.

Anonymous or aggregated analytics information that can no longer reasonably be associated with your account may remain in analytics systems.
